Requirements:
FPGA PN: AMD/XILINX XC7A25T-2CSG325I
2 1/4'' Female connectors
16-bit ADC with 50kHz sampling rate.
16-bit DAC with 50kHz
Low pass filter land pattern for ADC input and DAC output
Power input must be Barrel jack
JTAG Programming headers must be exposed
12 IO pins with switches
ON/OFF Switch for the system

Wants:
On-board USB-UART bridge
QSPI Flash can maybe store different configurations for the user
ADC and DAC with output clock for synchronization with FPGA (connect them to 2 IOs)
 Ideally an extra power source 9V Alkaline battery
To choose the power source, ideally we can do it automatically, but can implement it with a switch
Size less than 100mm x 100mm
4-layer board (probably won't be met, and will be 6 layer)
